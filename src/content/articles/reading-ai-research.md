This is sample content for the Pulsar scaffold, not a report of a current event.

## Start with the question

Before reading a headline, identify the question the researchers set out to answer.
A benchmark score can be useful evidence, but its meaning depends on the task,
the data, and the conditions of the evaluation.

## Follow the evidence

Look for the original paper, a description of the evaluation, and acknowledged
limitations. Keep measured results separate from predictions about future uses.

## Keep a useful note

A short reading note can capture the research question, the result, and one open
question. Future Pulsar articles can build on this format with primary sources.
